# Bike Sharing Dataset

Hadi Fanaee-T (2013), Bike Sharing, UCI Machine Learning Repository.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5W894
Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/275/bike+sharing+dataset
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The daily `day.csv` file contains 731 observations from 2011–2012. The official file is included unchanged, with its line endings and header preserved. A SHA256 of its BOM-free, LF-normalized, trimmed UTF-8 text identifies the accepted source revision. Student reports and business briefs are authored by PractiSQL; they are not UCI endorsements.

`cnt` is total daily rentals (`casual` + `registered`). `yr` is 0 for 2011 and 1 for 2012. `mnth` is month, 1–12. `weathersit` is the documented weather-condition category code; this observational dataset does not establish causal effects. We retain the source codes and do not reinterpret disputed season labels.
