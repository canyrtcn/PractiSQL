[English](../en/screenshots.md) | [Türkçe](../tr/screenshots.md)

All screenshots use the English interface. Browse 30 sections in Light, Leaf and Dark. The creator’s name and photo are included with explicit permission. Learning statistics are synthetic; personal databases and conversations are excluded.

[Gallery / Galeri](../showcase/index.html?lang=en) · [Presentation visuals / Sunum görselleri](../showcase/index.html?lang=en#presentation)

Retail records and workspace names are synthetic. Built-in lesson/challenge fixtures and the documented UCI bike-sharing source appear in their own sections. The teaching message and exam material are prepared demo content, rather than a live model score. Query results, challenge checks and project numeric results were executed locally. Study-note screenshots show the actual application-generated print document.
