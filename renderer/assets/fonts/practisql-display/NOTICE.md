# PractiSQL Display

A custom display typeface based on Titan One, designed by Rodrigo Fuenzalida. The nine characters in the PractiSQL wordmark reproduce the lettering of the project’s generated promotional artwork as vector outlines. Other glyphs retain Titan One’s design, with a dotted capital I added for Turkish. All Turkish letters are supported. This is an adaptation, not a claim that the illustration was typeset in an identified commercial font.
